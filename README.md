## Hello there! 👋

I'm Gabriele!  

I am an astrophysicist and a PhD candidate at **Centro de Estudios de Física del Cosmos de Aragón (CEFCA)**.

My research interests in astrophysics primarily lie in the areas of minor bodies and planetary science. I'm curious about using Bayesian statistics, data analysis, and machine learning techniques in the field of astrophysics. I'm also fascinated by the power of planetary geology and how it can help solve mysteries related to asteroids, comets, and rocky planets.

I'm also a science communicator for the [Link2Universe](https://t.me/L2U_official) outreach project.
